## Common Icon Patterns

Icons often come in pairs for state representation:

| Pattern | On/Active | Off/Inactive | Disabled |
|---------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Audio | `icAudioOn` | `icAudioOff` | `icAudioOnDisabled` / `icAudioOffDisabled` |
| Locks | `icLocksOn` | `icLocksOff` | `icLocksOnDisabled` / `icLocksOffDisabled` |
